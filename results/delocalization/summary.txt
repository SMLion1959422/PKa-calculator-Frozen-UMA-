== CHEMICALLY COHERENT PATTERN ==
The effect is weakest in carboxylic acids (655 rows, the LARGEST family) and
strongest in phenols. That is what the chemistry predicts: a carboxylate's
charge sits on its two oxygens almost regardless of substituent, so there is
little within-family variation in delocalization to detect. Phenolates vary
enormously (p-nitro vs p-methoxy), so there is a lot. The descriptor finds
signal exactly where chemical variation exists.

== STATISTICAL CAVEAT (important) ==
Rows are CLUSTERED BY MOLECULE: each molecule contributes up to 5 rows (one
per solvent), but the F-tests above treat rows as independent. The nominal
p-values are therefore anticonservative, roughly by the cluster size (~5).

Crude correction (divide F by ~5, refit dof to molecule counts):
  phenol    uma_F   333.9 -> 66.8   still p=2.7e-10  SURVIVES
  N-H acid  uma_IPR  47.3 ->  9.5   still p=4.5e-03  SURVIVES
  S-H acid  uma_IPR  58.8 -> 11.8   p=0.01           borderline
  C-H acid  uma_R    32.8 ->  6.6   p=0.03           does not survive
  carbox.   uma_F    11.9 ->  2.4   p=0.13           does not survive
  other O-H uma_IPR   8.8 ->  1.8   p=0.24           does not survive

So the honest claim is: the effect is established in PHENOLS and N-H ACIDS,
borderline in S-H, and not established elsewhere. A molecule-level
permutation test (now implemented, --n-perm) replaces this crude correction
with a valid p-value and MUST be run before any of this is quoted.

Other limitations: 7 descriptors x 6 families = 42 tests, so a multiple-
comparison correction is also needed; three families have <20 molecules;
and LOFO shows the descriptors do not transfer to an unseen family.

READING:
* The within-family effect is REAL. It is not pseudo-replication.
* UMA is the best descriptor family, and beats Gasteiger decisively in
  N-H acids and S-H acids (3/3 vs 0/3 surviving Holm).
* BUT a trivial conjugated-atom count (topo_conj) survives in 4/6, matching
  two of the three UMA descriptors. UMA's advantage over the CHEAPEST
  baseline is real but modest when counted this way; it is larger when
  measured by effect size (phenol F 334 vs 75; S-H F 59 vs 13).
* Carboxylic acids: nothing survives, in the largest family (131 molecules).
  Chemically consistent - carboxylate delocalization barely varies with
  substituent, so there is little within-family signal to find.

REMAINING STATISTICAL WORK:
1. The p values printed as 0.0005 are AT the permutation floor (1/2001), so
   resolution is limited. Strict Bonferroni at alpha=0.01 over 42 tests needs
   p < 2.4e-4, below that floor: rerun the headline families with
   --n-perm 10000 to resolve.
2. The "UMA adds over Gasteiger" test (F 24.3, p 4.6e-11) is STILL an
   ordinary F-test and is NOT corrected for molecule clustering. It must be
   permuted the same way before being quoted.

THE ASYMMETRY IS DECISIVE:
  UMA adds over the conjugation count          F 57.1, p 0.0001
  the conjugation count does NOT add over UMA  F  2.1, p 0.21

UMA SUBSUMES the trivial baseline. The reason topo_conj survived in 4/6
families is that it captures a SUBSET of what UMA captures, not something
UMA misses. This was the open question after the 2000-permutation run and
it is now closed.

UMA also adds over Gasteiger AND the conjugation count TOGETHER (F 34.5,
p 0.0001) - the strictest test run.

UMA and Gasteiger are mutually complementary (each adds over the other),
so they encode different partial information; neither subsumes the other.

== WHAT IS NOW ESTABLISHED ==
1. A molecule's sensitivity to solvent H-bond donation is predicted by how
   far its UMA embedding response spreads from the ionization site.
2. This is NOT the family label: it holds within families, where the label
   is constant.
3. This is NOT pseudo-replication: molecule-level permutation confirms it.
4. This is NOT obtainable from a trivial conjugation count: UMA subsumes
   that baseline asymmetrically.
5. It is NOT reducible to Gasteiger charges either, though the two are
   complementary rather than one dominating.
6. Carboxylic acids remain the exception (nothing survives), which is
   chemically expected: carboxylate delocalization barely varies with
   substituent, so there is no within-family signal to find.

This is the clearest evidence in the project that frozen foundation-model
embeddings carry chemistry a cheap descriptor does not.
